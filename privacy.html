Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Secretaria Vinicius ("the app") is a personal tool built and used only by its developer, Vinicius Ramos, for his own Google accounts.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the app uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) to read the owner's own email messages, such as bills and appointments.

How the data is used

Message content is used only to identify due dates, bills and appointments so they can be added to the owner's own calendar and shown to the owner as reminders.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Email content is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine learning models. It is processed only to produce the owner's reminders. The app cannot send, delete or modify email.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
